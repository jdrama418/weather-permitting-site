Weather Permitting

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.
  • Your plans stay on your device. We don't run any servers, so nothing is sent to us.
  • To get forecasts, each plan's location is sent to Apple Weather. Place searches go to Apple Maps.
  • Your location is used only when you tap Current location, and only while the app is open.

What the app stores, and where

Weather Permitting keeps everything on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Your plans: each plan's name, type, indoor or outdoor setting, place (its name, address, coordinates and time zone), times, repeat rule, theme, and any dates you skip.
  • Forecasts saved for those plans, so the app and widgets can show them without going online.
  • Your answers to "Was this right?" after a plan ends, and your unit settings.

These are stored in the app's own storage and in a shared container on the device that only Weather Permitting and its widgets can read. They aren't uploaded anywhere by the app. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, the backup may include this data, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Apple and your own settings, not by us.

Coming later: iCloud sync. A future version will sync your plans between your devices through your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud, in your private database; we can't see it). This policy will be updated before that version is released.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you tap Current location while choosing a place for a plan. It asks for permission to use location only while the app is in use, never in the background. It takes one reading, turns it into a place name with Apple Maps, saves that place with the plan, and stops. If you'd rather not share your location, you can search for any place instead; the app works fully without location access.

Searching for places

When you search for a place, what you type is sent to Apple Maps to suggest matching places. Tapping a plan's place opens it in Apple Maps. Apple handles these requests under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Weather

Forecasts come from Apple Weather (WeatherKit). To get them, the app sends Apple each plan's coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, along with the dates it needs. It doesn't send plan names or anything else about the plan. This happens when you open the app and, if Background App Refresh is on, now and then in the background. The app also downloads the Apple Weather logo from Apple so it can show the required credit. Apple handles these requests under Apple's Privacy Policy.

No data goes to servers run by us; there aren't any.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking, and no third-party code or SDKs.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Feedback emails

If you tap Send feedback in Settings, the app opens an email draft in your mail app. The draft includes the app's version and build, your iOS version, and whether you're on an iPhone or iPad, so you can see exactly what's sent before you send it. If you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and we don't share it. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included, so there's nothing special collected from children.

Deleting your data

Delete a plan in the app (swipe left on it, or tap Delete Plan when editing it) and the plan and its saved forecast are removed. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. You can turn off location access at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes (or the TestFlight notes during the beta) for the version it applies to.
