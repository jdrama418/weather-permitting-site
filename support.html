What Weather Permitting does

Weather Permitting watches the weather for the plans you care about, like a birthday in the park, Saturday's game or the walk to school, and tells you how much the weather will affect each one. Save a plan once with its place and time, and the forecast for exactly that window lives on your Home Screen in a widget.

Each plan gets a Weather Impact rating (Minimal, Light, Moderate or Heavy) and a one-line suggestion, such as "Bring jackets." More than 10 days out, a plan shows the usual weather for that date; as the day gets closer, the real forecast arrives and sharpens to hour by hour. Repeating plans always show their next time.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap a quick start (or Add a plan).
  2. Give the plan a name, pick a type and a place, and set the time.
  3. Add a widget: touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for Weather Permitting. The app shows these steps again in Settings › Add a widget.

Common questions

The forecast says it couldn't update

The app keeps the last forecast it saved and shows how old it is. Check your connection and tap Try again, or pull down on the plans list to refresh.

A widget looks out of date

Widgets show the forecast the app last saved. Opening the app refreshes it; with Background App Refresh on, it also refreshes on its own from time to time.

Current location doesn't work

Location access may be off. Turn it on in the Settings app under Privacy & Security › Location Services › Weather Permitting, or simply search for the place instead.
